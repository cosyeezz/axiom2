## Scope and limitations

These are separate bounded fixture sets, not a summed count of HTTP/SDK requests or subprocesses. No official Kiro/model/authentication/network experiment was performed by these stream tests. They neither authorize the guarded runner nor prove transparency of the original HTTP API, normal login, no UI, full descendants, server receipt, model availability or parity.

`complete` means stream EOF plus available direct-child exit and no capture fault; it is not exit0 or business success. Unknown stream bytes, hashes, argv/env and arbitrary exception text are not component outputs. This is metadata projection, not secure erasure: `from None` hides rendering but retains context/locals, and clearing tails does not erase every chunk/window. Cleanup fault simulations do not prove real pre-release close failure, failed kill, reap timeout, BaseException, kernel faults or descendant containment. Parent and child of the future real-profile runner must not inherit/set a low FSIZE limit on normal SQLite; the component deliberately sets no FSIZE limit.
